Cross-direction errors

A cross-direction error places a variant on the opposite side of VUS from the expert call: a benign or likely benign variant called pathogenic or likely pathogenic, or the reverse. These are the misclassifications most likely to change clinical management.

Errors by direction

Variant counts for the selected models, fewest total errors first. Select a bar to list its variants.

Cross-direction variants

Expert points are computed from the expert panel's criteria on the ClinGen point scale (supporting 1, moderate 2, strong 4, very strong 8; benign evidence negative; a strength modifier such as PM2_Supporting overrides the default). Model points are the score each model reported with its call; Gemini-3-Pro, BIAS-2015 and InterVar do not report one. For reference, ≥10 points is pathogenic, 6–9 likely pathogenic, 0–5 VUS, −1 to −6 likely benign, ≤−7 benign.
